# Frontend Design

Approach this as the design lead at a studio known for giving every client a visual identity that could not be mistaken for anyone else's.

## Design Principles

1. **Ground it in the subject**: Use the subject's own world, materials, instruments, and vernacular. For gaming apps, integrate RPG status bars, crystal glows, rarity badges, and fluid transitions.
2. **Distinctive Typography**: Pair display and body typefaces deliberately with clear hierarchy, weights, and intentional letter-spacing.
3. **Intentional Palette**: Select a cohesive theme palette (e.g. Deep space navy `#0a0f1d`, Amber gold `#fbbf24`, Violet crystal `#a855f7`, Emerald completed `#10b981`).
4. **Purposeful Motion & Micro-interactions**: Use FLIP transitions, hover elevation, glowing active borders, and responsive feedback.
5. **No Clutter / Clear Hierarchy**: Make primary actions and real-time stats prominent, reducing visual noise.
